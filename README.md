I design and lead delivery of cloud-native .NET systems, and for the last while I've been working out where AI genuinely fits into them. 15+ years in, I still learn most by building small, honest reference projects.

## How I approach architecture

- **Boundaries before frameworks.** Get the domain and dependency direction right first; tools are replaceable.
- **Observable by default.** Logging and tracing are design inputs, not afterthoughts.
- **Boring where possible.** Spend novelty on the problem, not the plumbing.
